# Giant challenge sheet: Bible camp edition

Print copies of this document for each challenge judge table. Helpers do not need prior prep. Every Bible question includes the exact answer printed below it.

---

## Instructions for challenge judges

1. **Keep the line moving.** Each team attempt should take under 45 seconds. If a camper struggles or fails, say: "Great try! Head back, practice with your team, and try again or pick another one."
2. **One completion per challenge per team.** When a team completes challenge #14, mark #14 on their punch card. They cannot repeat #14 for more chips.
3. **Age matching.** Direct younger campers (ages 7-11) toward Tier 1 and creative tasks. Encourage teenagers to tackle Tier 2 and Tier 3.
4. **Instant payout.** Hand poker chips directly to the camper the moment they succeed.

---

## Tier 1: Easy challenges (Payout: 2 poker chips each)
Great for younger campers (ages 7-11) and quick warm-ups.

### 1. The ark builder
- **Task:** Who built the ark in the book of Genesis?
- **Answer:** Noah (Genesis 6).

### 2. Walk on water
- **Task:** Name the two people in the Bible who walked on water.
- **Answer:** Jesus and Peter (Matthew 14:29). Accept just Peter or Jesus if camper is under 9.

### 3. Giant slayer
- **Task:** What was the name of the giant that young David defeated with a sling and a stone?
- **Answer:** Goliath (1 Samuel 17).

### 4. Big fish tale
- **Task:** Which prophet was swallowed by a great fish when he tried to run away to Tarshish?
- **Answer:** Jonah (Jonah 1).

### 5. Name two disciples
- **Task:** Name any two of Jesus' twelve disciples.
- **Answer:** Any two of: Peter, Andrew, James, John, Philip, Bartholomew, Thomas, Matthew, James son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Zealot, Judas Iscariot.

### 6. Two commandments
- **Task:** Name any two of the Ten Commandments.
- **Answer:** Any two of:
  1. No other gods before God.
  2. Do not make or worship idols.
  3. Do not misuse the Lord's name.
  4. Remember the Sabbath day.
  5. Honor your father and mother.
  6. Do not murder.
  7. Do not commit adultery.
  8. Do not steal.
  9. Do not lie (false testimony).
  10. Do not covet.

### 7. City of birth
- **Task:** In what small town was Jesus born?
- **Answer:** Bethlehem (Luke 2).

### 8. The parted waters
- **Task:** What body of water did God part through Moses so the Israelites could escape Egypt?
- **Answer:** The Red Sea (Exodus 14).

### 9. Shortest verse
- **Task:** Recite the shortest verse in the English Bible (John 11:35).
- **Answer:** "Jesus wept."

### 10. Sing "Jesus loves me"
- **Task:** Sing the first verse and chorus of "Jesus Loves Me" out loud with enthusiasm.
- **Pass condition:** Must sing clearly without stopping midway.

### 11. Sing "This little light of mine"
- **Task:** Sing the chorus of "This Little Light of Mine" while holding up a pointer finger candle and blowing it out at the end.
- **Pass condition:** Must do the finger action.

### 12. Pencil balance trick
- **Task:** Balance an unsharpened pencil upright on the tip of one finger for 7 full seconds without dropping it.
- **Pass condition:** Count to 7 out loud.

### 13. High-five speed run
- **Task:** Run and give 5 high-fives to 5 people from 5 different teams in under 15 seconds.
- **Pass condition:** Must name each person or team as they tag.

### 14. Draw your boat
- **Task:** On an index card or scratch paper, draw your team's boat on water with a flag, sail, and smiley face in 20 seconds.
- **Pass condition:** Judge inspects and accepts the drawing.

### 15. The standing stork
- **Task:** Stand on one foot with eyes closed and hands on hips for 10 seconds without falling over.
- **Pass condition:** Eyes must stay closed the entire count.

## Tier 2: Medium challenges (Payout: 4 poker chips each)
Good for mixed age pairs and energetic teens.

### 16. The four gospels
- **Task:** Name the four Gospel books in the New Testament in order.
- **Answer:** Matthew, Mark, Luke, John.

### 17. The boy's lunch
- **Task:** What food did the boy offer Jesus before Jesus fed the 5,000 people?
- **Answer:** 5 loaves of bread and 2 small fish (John 6:9).

### 18. Recite John 3:16
- **Task:** Recite John 3:16 from memory.
- **Answer:** "For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life." (Accept slight wording variations based on translation).

### 19. Recite Genesis 1:1
- **Task:** Recite the very first verse of the Bible.
- **Answer:** "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."

### 20. The first five books
- **Task:** Name the first 5 books of the Old Testament (the Pentateuch / Torah) in order.
- **Answer:** Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy.

### 21. Lions' den survivor
- **Task:** Who was thrown into the lions' den for praying to God, and why was he unharmed?
- **Answer:** Daniel. God sent an angel to shut the lions' mouths (Daniel 6).

### 22. Coat of many colors
- **Task:** Which son of Jacob received an ornate robe (coat of many colors) from his father and was later sold into slavery in Egypt?
- **Answer:** Joseph (Genesis 37).

### 23. Four fruits of the Spirit
- **Task:** Name any 4 of the 9 fruits of the Spirit listed in Galatians 5.
- **Answer:** Any four of: Love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control.

### 24. Judas and the coins
- **Task:** How many pieces of silver did Judas Iscariot accept to betray Jesus?
- **Answer:** 30 pieces of silver (Matthew 26:15).

### 25. The bottle flip
- **Task:** Take a standard half-filled plastic water bottle and land one successful upright bottle flip onto the table.
- **Pass condition:** Camper gets up to 3 attempts per turn. If they fail all 3, they must return to their team before trying again.

### 26. Ping pong cup bounce
- **Task:** Bounce a ping pong ball on the table once and make it land inside a red Solo cup from at least 4 feet away.
- **Pass condition:** 3 attempts allowed.

### 27. Coin toss drop
- **Task:** Drop a poker chip from chin height and have it land directly into a plastic cup sitting on the floor.
- **Pass condition:** 3 attempts allowed.

### 28. Backwards ABC sprint
- **Task:** Say the alphabet backwards from Z to P without pausing for more than 2 seconds.
- **Answer:** Z, Y, X, W, V, U, T, S, R, Q, P.

### 29. Draw Noah's ark in 30 seconds
- **Task:** Draw Noah's ark with at least 3 recognizable pairs of animals visible on board within 30 seconds.
- **Pass condition:** Helper must be able to name the three animals.

### 30. Marshmallow toss
- **Task:** Two teammates stand 6 feet apart. One tosses a regular marshmallow, and the other catches it cleanly in their mouth.
- **Pass condition:** 3 toss attempts allowed.

## Tier 3: Hard challenges (Payout: 6 poker chips each)
Designed for older youth (ages 12-18) and serious scripture memory or teamwork.

### 31. Recite Psalm 23:1-3
- **Task:** Recite the first 3 verses of Psalm 23 from memory.
- **Answer:** "The Lord is my shepherd, I lack nothing. He makes me lie down in green pastures, he leads me beside quiet waters, he refreshes my soul. He guides me along the right paths for his name's sake."

### 32. All nine fruits of the Spirit
- **Task:** Recite all 9 fruits of the Spirit without looking at a Bible.
- **Answer:** Love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control (Galatians 5:22-23).

### 33. Six tribes of Israel
- **Task:** Name any 6 of the 12 sons or tribes of Israel.
- **Answer:** Any six of: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Dan, Naphtali, Gad, Asher, Issachar, Zebulun, Joseph (or Ephraim/Manasseh), Benjamin.

### 34. Old Testament "J" books
- **Task:** Name 4 books of the Old Testament that start with the letter J.
- **Answer:** Joshua, Judges, Job, Jeremiah, Joel, Jonah (any 4).

### 35. Recite Philippians 4:13 and Romans 6:23
- **Task:** Recite both of these verses word for word.
- **Answer:**
  - Philippians 4:13: "I can do all this through him who gives me strength."
  - Romans 6:23: "For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord."

### 36. Seven disciples speed test
- **Task:** Name 7 of Jesus' 12 disciples in under 15 seconds.
- **Answer:** Peter, Andrew, James, John, Philip, Bartholomew, Thomas, Matthew, James son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Zealot, Judas.

### 37. The days of creation
- **Task:** Name what God created on Days 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 in Genesis 1.
- **Answer:**
  - Day 1: Light / Day and night.
  - Day 2: Sky / Waters separated.
  - Day 3: Dry land, seas, plants and trees.
  - Day 4: Sun, moon, and stars.
  - Day 5: Sea creatures and birds.
  - Day 6: Land animals and humans.

### 38. The plagues of Egypt
- **Task:** Name any 5 of the 10 plagues of Egypt from Exodus.
- **Answer:** Any five of: Water to blood, frogs, gnats/lice, flies, livestock disease, boils, hail and fire, locusts, darkness, death of the firstborn.

### 39. The triple bottle flip
- **Task:** Two teammates must both flip a water bottle at the same time and land both upright simultaneously.
- **Pass condition:** Up to 4 attempts allowed per turn.

### 40. Blindfolded chip recovery
- **Task:** One teammate is blindfolded. A second teammate stands 8 feet away and gives only verbal directions to guide the blindfolded player across the room to pick up a single poker chip off the floor.
- **Pass condition:** No physical contact allowed. Must complete in under 45 seconds.

### 41. Ping pong bounce wall trick
- **Task:** Bounce a ping pong ball off the floor, off a flat wall, and catch it in a plastic cup held in your hand.
- **Pass condition:** 3 attempts allowed.

### 42. Card tower engineering
- **Task:** Using 6 index cards, build a free-standing tower at least 2 stories high that stands untouched for 5 seconds.
- **Pass condition:** 60-second build limit.

### 43. 20 push-ups while quoting Scripture
- **Task:** A camper does 20 clean push-ups while their teammate counts and quotes Joshua 1:9 out loud.
- **Pass condition:** "Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the Lord your God will be with you wherever you go."

### 44. Memory chain speed run
- **Task:** Three teammates recite the books of the New Testament from Matthew through Galatians, alternating one book per person in order.
- **Answer:** Matthew, Mark, Luke, John, Acts, Romans, 1 Corinthians, 2 Corinthians, Galatians.

### 45. Human knot untangle
- **Task:** Four campers from the team form a human knot with crossed hands and untangle themselves into an open circle in under 30 seconds without letting go of hands.
- **Pass condition:** Complete circle formed.

## Tier 4: Creative and spirit challenges (Payout: 5 poker chips each)
High-energy, team-building tasks judged on effort and enthusiasm.

### 46. The 30-second boat commercial
- **Task:** Two campers present a dramatic 30-second sales pitch to the judge explaining why their boat is completely unsinkable.
- **Pass condition:** Must include a catchy slogan, a demonstration gesture, and loud confidence.

### 47. The team cheer and anthem
- **Task:** At least 3 teammates perform a 4-line rhyming team chant complete with coordinated claps or stomps.
- **Pass condition:** All members must participate and chant in unison.

### 48. Helper portrait sketch
- **Task:** Draw a recognizable, funny portrait of one of the college helpers or the camp director on an index card in 45 seconds.
- **Pass condition:** The helper being drawn must look at it and laugh or approve.

### 49. The robot boat dance
- **Task:** Two campers do the mechanical robot dance for 20 seconds while making engine and propeller noises.
- **Pass condition:** Total commitment to the dance.

### 50. Group frozen statue
- **Task:** Four teammates pose as a ship crew battling a massive ocean storm and hold completely motionless for 15 seconds.
- **Pass condition:** No laughing, twitching, or moving during the 15-second count.

### 51. The pirate prayer
- **Task:** Recite a 3-sentence prayer thanking God for camp, friends, and boats in a full pirate accent ("Ahoy Lord...", "Shiver me timbers...").
- **Pass condition:** Must stay in character through the "Amen, matey!"

### 52. Animal sound symphony
- **Task:** Three teammates each pick a different animal from Noah's ark and perform the chorus of a worship song using only their animal noise (example: bark, moo, quack).
- **Pass condition:** Keep going for 15 seconds without stopping.

### 53. Camp director handshake
- **Task:** Create an elaborate 6-move secret handshake with a judge, practice it, and perform it smoothly once through.
- **Pass condition:** Must include at least two snaps, a fist bump, and an elbow tap.
