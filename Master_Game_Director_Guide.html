48 Campers: 8-Team Allocation
Roster: 13 children (7-11) + 35 teens (12-18) = 48 kids.
Team breakdown (exactly 6 per team):
  • 5 teams: 2 younger kids (7-11) + 4 teens (12-18)
  • 3 teams: 1 younger kid (7-11) + 5 teens (12-18)
Mandatory roles at each table (6 campers):
  • 1 Lead Builder: Manages scissors and hull assembly.
  • 2 Challenge Runners: Sprint to earn chips.
  • 1 Treasurer: Guards chips and visits store.
  • 2 Quality Techs: Cut tape and test waterproof seals.
Testing Arena Rules (1 Tank)
1. Setup: 18-gallon clear plastic tub filled 2/3 with water on sturdy table with towels.
2. Buoyancy check (5s): Boat must float on its own without human touch for 5 seconds empty. If it flips with 0 weight, score = 0.
3. Adding weights: Captain places 1.5" fender washers (or 10-packs) gently into cargo hold every 2-3s.
4. Sinking definition: Water overflows gunwales, boat capsizes, or hull touches bottom of tub.
5. Official score: Total washers held for 3s before sink.

Award Categories
1. Heavyweight Champion: Boat holding highest total washers.
2. Master Engineer (ROI Award): Highest score of `Weights Held / Chips Spent`. Rewards smart low-cost design!
3. Most Creative Vessel: Voted by college helpers for name, visual flag, and structural ingenuity.
4. Sweet Treat Splurger: Team that bought the most snacks (fun mention).
Tie-Breaker Rule
If two boats hold the exact same number of weights:
  • Tie-breaker 1: The team that spent fewer chips at the shop wins.
  • Tie-breaker 2: If still tied, the team with the younger average age wins.

5-Minute Spiritual Application: Building On The Rock (Matthew 7)

"Everyone then who hears these words of mine and does them will be like a wise man who built his house on the rock. And the rain fell, and the floods came, and the winds blew and beat on that house, but it did not fall, because it had been founded on the rock." • Matthew 7:24-25

Message to campers: Look at what happened when heavy weights were loaded into your boats today. The boats that held the most were not always the flashiest. They had solid, balanced foundations, tight seals, and careful craftsmanship. In life, God gives each of you resources: time, energy, choices, and friendships. Some people spend everything on quick sweet treats. Those treats looked completely normal and appealing, but they drained resources away from the ship. That is how temptation works in our lives: it looks harmless and delightful, but it distracts us from what matters. Other teams worked hard on Bible challenges, memorized Scripture, bought solid materials, and planned ahead. In life, build your foundation on God's Word!
