Shopkeeper Operations Guide

Confidential Helper Instructions • Materials Store & Bank Management

Staff Only
Shop Station Setup (Do This First)
  • Table placement: Place your table with your back to supply boxes. Campers stay in front. Never let campers reach into boxes.
  • Sweet treats: Put them right in front of the counter in an attractive clean tray. Keep them looking appealing and completely normal.
  • Bank box: Keep the cash box (poker chip bank) behind the table next to your clipboard.
  • Scissors return bin: Place a box labeled "Returned Scissors" behind you.
Tape Dispensing Standard
Never hand a team a roll of tape. You measure and tear:
1 Pull tape from your fingertips to your shoulder (~2.5 feet).
2 Tear or cut cleanly with your scissors.
3 Stick the top 1/2 inch to table edge for the camper to grab.
Duct Tape = 5 Chips • Masking Tape = 3 Chips per strip.
Strict Closing Policy
Shop closes at exactly Minute 38.
Give a loud 5-minute warning at Minute 33. When the clock hits Minute 38, close the boxes and declare the shop closed. Even if a team begs for tape, hold the line. Engineering requires respecting the launch deadline!
The Subtle Temptation Dynamic
Pedagogy: In the real world, temptation never advertises itself as a trap. It looks harmless, pleasant, and appealing. Do NOT label treats as "bait" or warn campers.

Script when approached:
Camper: "Can I buy a treat?"
You: "Sure! 2 chips for regular, 3 for deluxe. Chips on the counter first!"

When an upset treasurer arrives:
Treasurer: "They spent our boat money on snacks! Can we get our chips back?"
You: "All sales are final. Part of teamwork and life is communicating with your team and managing your resources wisely. Send runners to earn chips back!"
Exchange & Return Rules
  • No returns: Used tape, crumpled foil, or cut cardboard are never accepted back.
  • Even swaps (First 15 min only): Uncut 500 mL bottles or undamaged craft sticks can be swapped for an item of equal or lesser value.
  • No cash refunds: Never hand poker chips back once paid into the shop bank.
