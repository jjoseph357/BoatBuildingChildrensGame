Instant Helper Answer Key • Mark Punch Card Upon Pass • Give Chips Immediately

STAFF KEY
1. Fast Pace Keep attempts under 45s. Send campers back to practice if stuck.
2. One Payout Initial punch card on completion. Teams cannot repeat challenges.
3. Memory Slips Point kids to verse text on this sheet so they can study with team!
4. Age Guide Ages 7-11: Tier 1. Ages 12-18: Tiers 2 & 3. Anyone: Tier 4.
Tier 1: Easy Challenges • Ages 7-11 Payout: +2 Poker Chips
#01. The Ark Builder +2 CHIPS
Who built the ark in the book of Genesis?
Key: Noah (Genesis 6).
#02. Walk On Water +2 CHIPS
Name two people who walked on water.
Key: Jesus and Peter (Matthew 14). Accept just Peter if age < 9.
#03. Giant Slayer +2 CHIPS
Who was the giant David defeated with a sling?
Key: Goliath (1 Samuel 17).
#04. Big Fish Tale +2 CHIPS
Which prophet was swallowed by a great fish?
Key: Jonah (Jonah 1).
#05. Name Two Disciples +2 CHIPS
Name any two of Jesus' twelve disciples.
Key: Peter, Andrew, James, John, Philip, Thomas, Matthew, etc.
#06. Two Commandments +2 CHIPS
Name any two of the Ten Commandments.
Key: Honor parents; Do not steal; Do not lie; No other gods; etc.
#07. City Of Birth +2 CHIPS
In what town was Jesus born?
Key: Bethlehem (Luke 2).
#08. The Parted Waters +2 CHIPS
What body of water did Moses part?
Key: The Red Sea (Exodus 14).
#09. Memory: John 11:35 +2 CHIPS
Memorize & recite shortest verse in English Bible.
Verse: "Jesus wept." (John 11:35).
#10. Memory: 1 John 4:19 +2 CHIPS
Memorize & recite 1 John 4:19.
Verse: "We love because he first loved us." (1 John 4:19).
#11. Sing "Jesus Loves Me" +2 CHIPS
Sing verse 1 and chorus out loud with enthusiasm.
Pass: Sings clearly through chorus without stopping.
#12. "This Little Light" +2 CHIPS
Sing chorus with pointer finger candle action.
Pass: Must hold finger up and blow it out at the end!
#13. Pencil Balance +2 CHIPS
Balance a pencil on 1 finger for 7 full seconds.
Pass: Count to 7 out loud. No touching with other hand.
#14. High-Five Sprint +2 CHIPS
High-five 5 kids from 5 different teams in <15s.
Pass: Judge counts out loud. Must tag 5 different teams.

#22. The First Five Books +4 CHIPS

#24. Many-Colored Coat +4 CHIPS

Key: Joseph (Genesis 37).
#25. Four Fruits Of Spirit +4 CHIPS

Key: Love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control.

#29. Draw Noah's Ark +4 CHIPS
Draw ark with 3 distinct animal pairs in 30s.

#30. Partner Cup Catch +4 CHIPS

Giant Challenge Bank • Tier 3 (Hard)

Teens & Older Campers • Scripture Memory & Coordination • Payout: +6 Chips

TIER 3 • +6 CHIPS
#31. Memory: Psalm 23:1-3 +6 CHIPS
Memorize & recite Psalm 23:1-3 from memory.
Verse: "The Lord is my shepherd, I lack nothing. He makes me lie down in green pastures, he leads me beside quiet waters, he refreshes my soul. He guides me along the right paths for his name's sake."
#32. Memory: Phil 4:13 & Rom 6:23 +6 CHIPS
Memorize & recite both verses word-for-word.
Verses: "I can do all this through him who gives me strength." (Phil 4:13) & "For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord." (Rom 6:23)
#33. Memory: Joshua 1:9 + 20 Push-Ups +6 CHIPS
20 clean push-ups while teammate recites Joshua 1:9.
Verse: "Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the Lord your God will be with you wherever you go."
#34. Memory: Romans 8:28 +6 CHIPS
Memorize & recite Romans 8:28 from memory.
Verse: "And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose." (Romans 8:28).
#35. All 9 Fruits Of Spirit +6 CHIPS
Recite all 9 fruits of the Spirit without looking.
Key: Love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control (Gal 5).
#36. Six Tribes Of Israel +6 CHIPS
Name any 6 of the 12 tribes/sons of Israel.
Key: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Dan, Naphtali, Gad, Asher, Issachar, Zebulun, Joseph, Benjamin.
#37. Four OT "J" Books +6 CHIPS
Name 4 Old Testament books starting with J.
Key: Any 4 of: Joshua, Judges, Job, Jeremiah, Joel, Jonah.
#38. Seven Disciples In 15s +6 CHIPS
Name 7 disciples in under 15 seconds.
Key: Peter, Andrew, James, John, Philip, Thomas, Matthew, Bartholomew, Simon, Judas, Thaddaeus.
#39. Days Of Creation +6 CHIPS
Name what God made on Days 1 through 6.
Key: 1: Light; 2: Sky/waters; 3: Land/plants; 4: Sun/moon/stars; 5: Fish/birds; 6: Animals/humans.
#40. Five Plagues Of Egypt +6 CHIPS
Name any 5 of the 10 Egyptian plagues.
Key: Blood, frogs, gnats, flies, livestock, boils, hail, locusts, darkness, firstborn.
#41. Double 500 mL Flip +6 CHIPS
2 teammates flip 500 mL bottles & land both upright!
Pass: Both bottles land upright on same throw. 4 tries.
#42. Blindfolded Navigation +6 CHIPS
Teammate verbally guides blindfolded player to chip.
Pass: Voice only. Pick up chip in <45s from 8ft away.
#43. Wall Bounce Cup Catch +6 CHIPS
Bounce ball off floor, off wall, catch in cup.
Pass: Ball caught inside cup cleanly. 3 tries.
#44. Index Card Tower +6 CHIPS
Build 2-story tower with 6 cards that stands 5s.
Pass: Stands untouched for 5s count. 60s build limit.
#45. Memory Chain Speed Run +6 CHIPS
3 teammates alternate books: Matt to Galatians.
Key: Matthew, Mark, Luke, John, Acts, Romans, 1 Cor, 2 Cor, Galatians.
